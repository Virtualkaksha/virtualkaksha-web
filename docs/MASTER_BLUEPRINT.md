# VirtualKaksha - Master Blueprint

# Vision

To build India's most trusted digital learning ecosystem where every student can access high-quality education regardless of location, language or financial background.

---

# Mission

VirtualKaksha aims to connect students, teachers and coaching institutes on one platform by providing high-quality study material, online learning tools and educator discovery.

---

- NCERT Solutions
